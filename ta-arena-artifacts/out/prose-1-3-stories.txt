A greeting is a precise exchange. Time apart sets how much is owed. Too little leaves a gap, and too much reads as a pitch.
If greetings were only manners, the amount would not matter. Sam sulked at “Hey.” because a short hello carries a debt, and manners don't come with one.
“He's just hard to please” sounds like a shrug, and then you stop trying. The debt stays unpaid, so his “Cool” will come again.
