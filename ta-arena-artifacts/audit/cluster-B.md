## 3-4 Cuff Links

The question was whether "transference" is the right term. It is not a free-standing term in the books. GPP ch. 2 (p. 11) gives Type I crossing (stimulus Adult to Adult, response Child to Parent) as typified by the classical transference reaction, and Type II (p. 12) as typified by counter-transference. TAP ch. 9 says the same and adds that mutual Parent and Child transactions run until one changes ego state. The replay shows a Boss and Kid pair plus both crossings, so the term, note and source now say crossed transaction, Types I and II.
