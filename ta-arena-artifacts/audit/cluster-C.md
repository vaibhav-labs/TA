# Audit, cluster C: games 5-1 to 5-6 and 6-1 to 6-6

Scope: `content/5-1.json` to `content/6-6.json`. Method: `books/AUDIT-BRIEF.md`, with every claim searched in the book texts, not taken from earlier summaries. Books were paraphrased, never quoted into a game.

## Conventions used in the evidence column

- **SPL** (Scripts People Live): the text has no printed page numbers, so "SPL p. N" is the N-th page of the PDF, counted by form feed in `books/spl.txt`. Chapter starts used: ch. 1 p. 41, ch. 2 p. 54, ch. 3 p. 62, ch. 4 p. 65, ch. 5 p. 78, ch. 6 p. 84, ch. 7 p. 103, ch. 8 p. 110, ch. 9 p. 122, ch. 10 p. 141, ch. 14 p. 174, ch. 20 p. 233, ch. 21 p. 240. The front matter (pp. 1 to 40) holds the foreword and introduction.
- **GPP** (Games People Play): printed page numbers (printed page is the PDF page minus one in the pages used).
- **TA Today**: only the front matter, the contents page and chapters 1 to 7 are readable. Chapter 1 ("What TA Is", Key ideas of TA, book pp. 3 to 6) is the only readable source for rackets, stamps, discounting, redefining and symbiosis. For every other chapter in this cluster (12 to 21) the evidence is the contents page plus the acknowledgements in the front matter. Chapter numbers are safe to cite; page numbers from TA Today are not given.
- Steiner's vocabulary differs from TA Today in four places that matter here: his "discount" is a crossed transaction (SPL p. 124), his "racket" is the basis for collecting stamps (SPL p. 53), he says "counterscript" where TA Today says counter-injunction, and he writes "decision" and "program" (redecision appears only in the foreword, SPL p. 16).

## Summary of what stays UNVERIFIABLE

These details live in TA Today chapters 8 onward, or in standard TA theory outside the readable text. The games keep only what the contents page and standard theory support.

- The names and the count of the four discount levels (existence, significance, change possibilities, personal abilities), 5-3 and 5-6. Contents page of ch. 18 confirms "levels (modes)" and the diagram only.
- The names of the four passive behaviours (doing nothing, overadaptation, agitation, incapacitation or violence), 5-4. Contents page of ch. 17 confirms "the four passive behaviors" only.
- Healthy versus unhealthy symbiosis and everything past the ch. 1 definition, 5-5.
- The OK Corral in adult life, 6-1 (ch. 12 contents only).
- The five drivers and their names, 6-2 (ch. 16 contents lists "the five allowers", "primary driver", "the miniscript").
- The twelve injunctions beyond the ones Steiner lists: "Don't belong" and "Don't be a child", 6-3 (ch. 14 contents: "Twelve injunctions").
- The six process scripts and their names, 6-5 (ch. 15 contents: "Six process scripts"). SPL does not contain them; a search for the six names and for "process script" in `spl.txt` finds nothing.
- The term "counter-injunction", 6-6 (not in SPL, ch. 13 contents says only "script messages and the script matrix").
