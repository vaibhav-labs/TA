[H2] 6. Full scenes
Every scene below is written to the depth Claude Code needs: setting, cast, what the scene reads from earlier scenes, the beats, every option with its meter effect and outcome, the mini-board if any, how the scene ends, Meera's debrief card, and the flags it writes. Option tags: real (best), ok, cold (hurts Them), cave (hurts You), hook (a trap move — feels good, then costs). Meter deltas are out of 100. Spot level shows the same scene with a one-tap recognition question at the marked beat instead of choices; Analyse level adds the written field at the end.
