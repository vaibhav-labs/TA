# Cast notes: src/content/levels

`src/content/levels/*.json` (the root React app, `you`) is a **separate fictional universe** from `ta-arena-artifacts/content/*.json`. It does not use the Sam/Maya/Daniel/Halden cast in `docs/CHARACTER-BIBLE.md`, and it should stay that way: this is an anthology of mostly self-contained short stories, one protagonist (`playAs`) per level, in the tradition of a short-story collection rather than a soap-opera continuity. Do not merge the two universes or rename characters to match GLOBAL-NAMES.md here; that mapping applies only to `ta-arena-artifacts/`.

Two kinds of level live here:

1. **Anthology one-offs.** A new protagonist and situation each time (Troy, Camellia, Mike, Jules, Imran, Vikram, Amar...). Write these as tight, self-contained short stories per `master-storyteller`: enter late, one key event, concrete telling details, no need to connect to any other level.
2. **Historical case-study dramatizations** (1-1 Elena/Teo, 1-2 Rosita). These fictionalize real research (Spitz's foundling-home studies, Levine's rat-pup studies) as period-set short fiction. Keep them grounded in the actual study the `source` field cites; invent only the human texture (names, dialogue, setting), never the science.

**The one deliberate recurring thread:** Priya, a team lead, appears across Worlds 2 to 5 (2-1, 3-1, 3-2, 3-3, 4-3, 5-1, 5-6), with a consistent team around her. Keep her arc coherent if you touch more than one of her levels: she starts reactive and conflict-avoidant, and her Worlds 4 to 5 levels should show her starting to name patterns (Formula G, discounts) in her own team rather than just absorbing them. Other names that recur more loosely (Troy, Noor, Camellia, Mike, Jordan) are not required to form one continuous story; treat each appearance as its own short story unless the existing text already links them.

When deepening a level here: respect `playAs`, `setting` and `source` as already set (don't swap protagonists or citations). Strengthen scene-opening, raise the stakes of the Play-round bait, sharpen the gap between Social and Psychological level in Analyse rounds, and write any missing `L3` the same way the level's existing `L1`/`L2` are written (same protagonist, same voice, same stakes, continuing the scene rather than starting a new one).
