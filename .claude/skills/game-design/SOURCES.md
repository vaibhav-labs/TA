# Sources and coverage

## Michael E. Moore, *Basics of Game Design* (A K Peters/CRC Press, 2011)
Read from the user's Drive copy. The text export covered **PDF pages 1–170**: front
matter, full table of contents, preface, **ch. 1** Making Games, **ch. 2** Game Play and
Game Mechanics, **ch. 3** Math and Logic in Games, **ch. 4** On Movement, **ch. 5** On
Combat, and **ch. 6** On Characters and Monsters up to the Entity Attribute (Monster)
Chart. These chapters are summarized and applied in detail in:
`concept-and-pitch.md`, `mechanics-framework.md` (§7–8), `probability-and-math.md`,
`movement-and-space.md`, `combat.md`, `characters-and-entities.md`,
`production-and-gdd.md`, and parts of `balancing-and-playtesting.md`.

**Not in the provided copy** (the export stopped at page 170): the rest of ch. 6
(Treasure Tables, Sports Game Characters), ch. 7 Items, ch. 8 Magicks and Technologies,
ch. 9 Puzzles, ch. 10 Storytelling, ch. 11 Designing Playfields, ch. 12 Interface
Design, and ch. 13, the Chris Taylor interview. For these, the references follow the
book's table of contents and apply the method of the earlier chapters plus general
practice. Those files say so at the top: `content-systems.md`,
`story-dialogue-interface.md`, and parts of `progression-and-levels.md` and
`characters-and-entities.md` §14. To complete them, provide the remaining pages (e.g. a
split PDF under 10 MB per part) and update those files.

Errata spotted: ch. 3 gives the Senet 1-in-16 outcomes as "16.7%". The correct figure is
6.25% (corrected in `probability-and-math.md`). Ch. 3 says "earned run average (ERA)"
where batting average is meant; the reasoning is unaffected.

## Ernest Adams & Joris Dormans, *Game Mechanics: Advanced Game Design* (New Riders, 2012)
No copy of this book was in the provided files. The skill uses its widely published
framework: the five mechanic types, discrete vs. continuous mechanics, emergence vs.
progression, structural sources of emergence, internal economy (resources; sources,
drains, converters, traders), feedback-loop characteristics, the Machinations diagram
language, the mechanics design-pattern library, simulation-based balancing, missions and
spaces with lock-and-key progression, and meaningful mechanics. These are paraphrased,
not quoted, in `mechanics-framework.md`, `internal-economy.md`, `design-patterns.md`,
`progression-and-levels.md`, and `balancing-and-playtesting.md`. If you add the book, check
the pattern definitions and Machinations element list against it.
