First Transaction

You've never heard of Transactional Analysis. Good. That's exactly who this was built for.

Five tiny games, five minutes each, in order.

Transactional Analysis (TA) is a way of looking at why people talk and act the way they do. It was built by psychiatrist Eric Berne in the 1950s–60s and is still used by therapists, coaches, and regular people trying to understand their own conversations better. No couch, no jargon up front: each game drops you straight into a real situation, asks you to make a call, and only then names the idea behind it.

Every game ends with two minutes to reflect on where the idea shows up in your own life, privately, just for you. Play them in order; each one leans on the last.
